The Real-Time Audit Workflow

How VeriNote processes speech and text to preserve the integrity of news

1

DOM Scanning

The Chrome extension parses text segments as you browse, matching them against registered database claims.

2

Search Grounded AI

For new claims, Google Gemini searches the web live to verify facts against official bureau data, generating source citations.

3

Consensus Rating

Decentralized citizen auditors vote on note usefulness, dynamically adjusting trust percentages across all online tabs.
