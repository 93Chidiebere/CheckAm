Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about CheckAM

How does the real-time AI fact-checking work? +
CheckAM uses Google Gemini's Search-Grounded AI. When you submit a statement, the system initiates a real-time search query on official databases, government statistics, and reputable news outlets to verify facts and output citations.
What are the daily audit limits on free accounts? +
Free tier accounts are limited to 10 live AI search queries per day. Database ledger searches are always unlimited. You can upgrade to premium anytime to unlock unlimited live AI audits.
How are community notes consensus ratings decided? +
Any registered user can rate notes as "Helpful" or "Not Helpful". The consensus rating is calculated from these scores, requiring broad agreement to mark statements as verified.
